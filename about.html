ABOUT

Understanding the dynamics of how people, systems and societies change over time and shape the future.

I am a socio-economic systems researcher working at the intersection of demographic change, social dynamics and policy analysis.

My work explores how population dynamics, institutions, behaviour and policy interact over time. I use modelling to make these relationships more visible and to support thoughtful, evidence informed and sustainable decisions.

SHORT BIO

From operations research to demographic systems

My academic background is in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research, but my research gradually moved toward questions that are deeply social: population change, fertility, migration, ageing, labour markets and the long term consequences of policy decisions.

During my doctoral research, I used System Dynamics to study Iran's population dynamics. Since then, my work has expanded across demographic and socio-economic systems, combining dynamic modelling, quantitative analysis and foresight with applied policy research.

I am particularly interested in problems where feedback, delays and unintended consequences make simple explanations insufficient. My aim is to make these structures more visible and to use modelling as a way to support learning, dialogue and better decision making.

BEYOND THE RESEARCH

The people and places that shape my work

I am the mother of Sara and Soren, who have profoundly changed what a meaningful life means to me. Watching them grow fills me with energy and gives my work an even deeper sense of purpose, to contribute, however I can, to a more sustainable and resilient future for the generations to come.

I grew up in Yazd, in central Iran, and later moved to Tehran for my master's and doctoral studies. Those years shaped much of my academic path and eventually led me toward demographic modelling and System Dynamics. More recently, my family and I moved south to Hormozgan, near the Persian Gulf, seeking a quieter and more livable environment. Living by the coast has made questions of place, sustainability and quality of life feel more tangible in everyday life.
